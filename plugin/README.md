# Postclick for Claude

Postclick by PPC.io helps you improve a landing page. Ask Claude what to fix first, whether the page matches your ad, what makes buyers hesitate, or for new copy and a brief for your designer.

## What you get

Five skills. Claude picks the right one from what you ask.

| Skill | Ask something like |
|---|---|
| `postclick-fix-first` | "My landing page is not converting. What should I fix first?" |
| `postclick-message-match` | "Does this page match my Google ad?" |
| `postclick-buyer-objections` | "Why do people visit but not book?" |
| `postclick-copy-rewrite` | "Rewrite my hero and button." |
| `postclick-experiment-brief` | "Write a brief my designer can build from." |

The plugin also connects Claude to the Postclick server at `https://mcp.ppc.io`.

## Two ways to use it

**With your own page copy.** Paste the copy, a screenshot or your ad. The skills work on what you give Claude. You do not need a Postclick account.

**With a Postclick account.** The first time Claude uses the server, you sign in to Postclick to connect your account. Then Claude can read your saved audits, designs and reports. Reading saved work uses no credits. New audits, designs and page builds cost credits. Claude checks the price and asks you before it starts one.

## What the plugin sends

The skills are instructions for Claude. They run no code and send nothing on their own.

When Claude uses the Postclick server, it sends your request to `https://mcp.ppc.io` over HTTPS. That can include a page URL, page copy, ad copy and your questions. The server acts on your own Postclick account after you sign in. Postclick does not manage ad accounts.

Privacy policy: <https://ppc.io/privacy>. Terms: <https://ppc.io/terms>.

## Limits

Buyer reactions in an audit are modelled, not taken from real customers. Suggested changes need testing. Postclick does not guarantee conversion gains.
